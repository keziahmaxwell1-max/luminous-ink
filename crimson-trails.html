Synopsis

In the glittering high-society circles of Veridian, power is currency, trust is dangerous, and secrets rarely stay buried.

A string of calculated assassinations sends the city's elite into a spiral of fear. Each crime bears the same unsettling signature: the Veridian Thorn.

As suspicion spreads and carefully guarded secrets begin to surface, Dr. Tirian Maxwell finds himself drawn into a web of deception where appearances are rarely what they seem.

With danger closing in and loyalties growing uncertain, the line between truth and betrayal becomes increasingly difficult to recognise.

In Veridian, everyone has something to hide. And some secrets are worth killing for.
