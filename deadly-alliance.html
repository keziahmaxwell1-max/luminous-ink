Deadly Alliance

Mafia · Romance · Suspense

In a world built on betrayal, trust can be the deadliest mistake.

Synopsis

A dangerous assignment takes an unexpected turn, drawing two powerful forces into a game neither can afford to lose.

What begins with deception soon spirals into a tangled web of secrets, betrayal and unexpected alliances. With loyalties constantly shifting and danger lurking behind every decision, distinguishing friend from enemy becomes increasingly difficult.

In a world where trust is fragile and betrayal comes easily, one wrong choice could change everything.

Some alliances are formed for survival. Others become the very thing that destroys you.
