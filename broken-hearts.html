About the Book

She was once the jewel of a wealthy family, surrounded by luxury and promised a future filled with love. But one night of betrayal changed everything.

Stripped of her identity, sold into a world of cruelty and reduced to a pawn in a ruthless game of blood and debt, she lost everything she had ever known.

A tale of betrayal, cruelty, shattered dreams and the unexpected strength that can emerge from the darkest circumstances.

Some stories are far from over.
